# MAHABHARATA - EPISODE 1: "THE BEGINNING"

## SCENE 1: THE SAGE'S DILEMMA

*FADE IN:*

### EXT. HIMALAYAN FOOTHILLS - SUNSET

*Golden light bathes ancient mountains. A river winds through forests below.*

### EXT. ASHRAM - CONTINUOUS

*A modest wooden structure nestled in a clearing beside the river.*

### INT. ASHRAM - CONTINUOUS

*VYASA (60s, dark complexion, white hair and beard, intense eyes) sits surrounded by scrolls. He writes furiously, then stops, frustrated.*

**VYASA:**  
*(tossing down his quill)*  
Useless! The story moves faster than my hand can follow.

*He rises, walks to the window. The last rays of sun illuminate his troubled face.*

**VYASA:**  
This is not merely a tale of kings and wars. This is dharma itself - the path of righteousness that all must follow.

*He returns to his desk, places his palms on scattered manuscripts.*

**VYASA:**  
*(resolute)*  
The gods themselves must hear me. I require divine assistance.

*Vyasa sits in meditation position. The room darkens as he closes his eyes.*

**VYASA:**  
*(chanting softly)*  
Brahma, creator of all things, hear my plea. This knowledge must not die with me.

*A golden light begins to fill the room. Vyasa opens his eyes.*

*LORD BRAHMA appears, radiating divine light, seated on a lotus flower. Four-faced, golden-skinned, wearing elaborate divine garments.*

**BRAHMA:**  
*(voice resonating)*  
Why have you called me, Vyasa?

**VYASA:**  
*(bowing deeply)*  
Great Creator, I carry the history of our world within me - tales of valor, folly, and divine purpose. But my mortal hands cannot capture these visions quickly enough.

**BRAHMA:**  
*(thoughtfully)*  
The wisdom you seek to preserve is indeed precious. It requires a scribe of equal wisdom.

**VYASA:**  
Who among gods or mortals could perform such a task?

**BRAHMA:**  
*(smiling faintly)*  
Summon Lord Ganesha. His wisdom will comprehend your words, and his divine hand will record them faithfully.

*Brahma begins to fade.*

**BRAHMA:**  
But remember, Vyasa – divine assistance comes with divine conditions.

*Brahma disappears. Vyasa remains kneeling, his face illuminated by the fading divine light.*

**VYASA:**  
*(to himself)*  
So be it. The story of the Bharatas must be told, no matter the price.

*FADE TO BLACK.*

*Title appears: "MAHABHARATA"*

*FADE IN:*

### INT. ASHRAM - LATER THAT NIGHT

*Vyasa lights oil lamps around a small shrine.*

**VYASA:**  
*(chanting)*  
Lord Ganesha, remover of obstacles, hear my call...

*END SCENE*
